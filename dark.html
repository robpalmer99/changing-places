Our story

The same name, a new coastline

For twenty-two years, Changing Places Realty served buyers and sellers across the United States: multimillion-dollar homes, international clients, and the patient work of matching the right people to the right rooms.

That company has now come to Thailand. Peter Kelly brings the American experience; Nitiya Kelly (มิ้นท์) brings fluent Thai, local knowledge and the license on the wall. Together we work one small stretch of coast, from Naklua to Jomtien, so that every property we show is one we can drive you to within half an hour.

We focus on fine homes from ten million baht. Fewer clients, better homes, and a broker on your side of the table.

Time for a change.
