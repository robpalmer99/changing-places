# Changing Places Real Estate Company

register: brand

## What this is

Demo/brochure site for Changing Places Real Estate Company, a newly registered Thai
real estate agency in Pattaya run by Peter Kelly (American, 22 years running the
same-named brokerage in the US) and his wife Nitiya Kelly (มิ้นท์). Domain owned:
changingplacesrealty.com. Motto: "Time for a Change."

Built as a demonstration for Peter from the 2026-10 discovery call; listings are
illustrative placeholders until real inventory arrives.

## Users

~98% foreign buyers: American, European, Australian, some high-end Asian. Typically
retired or semi-retired couples browsing in the evening, often on tablets, dreaming
about or actively planning a move to Thailand. They are wealthy, unhurried, and
allergic to the hard-sell portal aesthetic.

## Positioning

- High-end sales only: 10M+ THB focus (~US$300k+), nothing under 2M THB.
- Geography: Pattaya ~5-mile radius. Naklua/Wongamat, Pratumnak (core), Jomtien,
  East Pattaya, Mabprachan Lake.
- Trust assets: 22 years of US brokerage experience, multimillion-dollar and
  international clients; bilingual Thai/English service (Nitiya Thai line,
  Peter English line).

## Primary job of the site

Lead capture. Facebook listing ads will land here; the one action that matters is
starting a conversation on WhatsApp or LINE. Email and phone are secondary. The
brochure/credibility role is secondary to that.

## Brand voice

Unhurried, polished, continental. Quiet-luxury resort register (Aman, Six Senses),
NOT property-portal register. Three words: dusk, bronze, patient.

## Anti-references

- FazWaz/DDproperty portal look: filter bars, badge spam, agent headshots in circles.
- Template realty sites: teal+orange, Poppins, "WE SELL YOUR HOME FAST" energy.
- Generic AI landing page: centered stack, icon cards, gradient text.
