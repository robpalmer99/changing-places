Seven property websites worth a scroll

Peter — these are real estate sites with strong, modern design: four from Pattaya, three national benchmarks. Have a scroll through them together on a laptop or tablet, and pick the two or three that feel most like the business you want to be. You don't need to explain why in design terms — "we like this one's photos" or "this one feels too busy" is exactly the feedback we need.
